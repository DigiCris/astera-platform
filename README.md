# Astera

### Regulated Tokenization Infrastructure

Astera is a compliance-native infrastructure layer for regulated digital asset issuance and trading.

Built for institutional-grade tokenization, Astera enables compliant digital asset markets through modular smart contract infrastructure, identity verification, compliance enforcement, regulated exchange flows, and on-chain auditability.

---

## Overview

Astera is designed to provide reusable infrastructure for regulated tokenized markets under compliant operational frameworks such as PSAVs, trust structures, regulated issuers, and permissioned financial platforms.

The platform separates:
- regulatory infrastructure,
- compliance enforcement,
- identity verification,
- and market operations,

from the frontend experience layer, allowing third parties to build compliant financial applications on top of Astera's infrastructure.

## Core Components

### Identity Registry
Maintains verified wallet-to-identity mappings and KYC approval status.

### Compliance Engine
Enforces transfer restrictions, market rules, limits, freezes, and permissioned operations.

### Security Tokens
Regulated digital assets representing participation rights, investment instruments, or tokenized financial products.

### Regulated Exchange Layer
Handles compliant issuance, purchase, and secondary market flows under controlled settlement rules.

### Audit & Event Layer
Provides immutable on-chain auditability through transparent event emissions and transaction history.

- scalable settlement,
